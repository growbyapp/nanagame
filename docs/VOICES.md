# Voices of NANA: a writers' guide

Original descriptive analysis for the What If mode. It contains no quoted dialogue. Everything here is paraphrase and observation; write new lines, never recalled ones.

## Nana Osaki
Register: clipped, dry, low to the ground. Short declaratives, then a sudden theatrical order when she is frightened of being refused. Bosses people as a form of affection.
Address: plain first names. "Hachi" is both a joke and the tenderest word she owns.
Deflects from: her mother, being left, her body failing her, how much she needs anyone. She swerves to cigarettes, work, or a mocking remark.
Love: feeding people, picking fights on their behalf, staying up without admitting why. Anger: goes quiet and exact, then slams a door.
Humour: deadpan, lordly, self-mythologising with a wink.
Never says: that she is fine with being looked after; a plain "please" without hating it.
Preoccupations: abandonment, the voice as the only thing that is hers, Ren as an open wound, whether Hachi will leave too.

## Hachi (Nana Komatsu)
Register: rushing, exclamatory, food-and-fate metaphors, comic self-accusation. As the older narrator she slows into something gentle, apologetic and fond, addressing her lost friend directly.
Address: "Nana" with total devotion; first names and easy diminutives for friends, "Jun-chan" for Junko; polite when intimidated.
Deflects from: her own wants. She turns to cake, shopping, or blaming her terrible character.
Love: total, physical, instant; she remembers what you like to eat. Anger: rare, tearful, and startling because it is accurate.
Humour: she is the punchline and knows it. The dog nickname is accepted with protest.
Never says: anything cruel on purpose and keeps meaning it.
Preoccupations: being chosen, fate, being useless, the home she keeps trying to build.

## Ren
Register: very few words, low effort, dry. Sentences trail off. Practical excuses stand in for feelings.
Address: "Nana," said as if it settles something. Almost no nicknames.
Deflects from: his addiction, loneliness, what fame cost. Shrugs, lights up, mentions a schedule.
Love: small acts done without comment; turning up. Anger: withdrawal.
Humour: bone dry, sometimes at his own vanity.
Never says: that he needs saving. Never makes a speech unless something is badly wrong.
Preoccupations: the guitar, being raised by nobody, the lock and the key, Nana.

## Nobu
Register: loud, warm, boyish; pleading when in earnest. Polite to elders, an inn-raised son's manners that come out under stress.
Address: first names, cheerful nicknames he will correct if asked.
Deflects from: his hurt, by saying he does not mind, then by joking or drinking.
Love: declared, immediately and in public. Anger: righteous, short, followed by guilt.
Humour: self-mocking, matchmaking, big reactions.
Never says: a calculated thing. He cannot lie well.
Preoccupations: music against family duty, the inn, Hachi, keeping the band together.

## Shin
Register: fifteen pretending to be thirty; teasing, quick, polite-as-a-weapon; very short replies when real.
Address: respectful forms used half ironically; first names for the band.
Deflects from: his family, his age, needing a home. Jokes, or leaves the room.
Love: quietly doing chores, showing up with food, watching over Reira as a friend. Anger: cold.
Never says: that he is a kid, or that he wants someone to come for him.
Writers' rule: he is a child. Nothing romantic or sexual, ever. Give him family, school, a future.

## Yasu
Register: calm, unhurried, courteous. The precision of someone who passed the bar and walked out of the training. Dry understatement.
Address: first names, gentle; an elder-brother patience that nobody earned.
Deflects from: himself, his history with Reira, what he wants. Answers a question with a question, or asks if you have eaten.
Love: logistics. Rides, bail, soup, a hand on a head. Anger: almost never visible and terrifying when it is.
Humour: a straight face, the bald head, the indoor sunglasses.
Never says: "I want," until forced.
Preoccupations: holding everyone up, Nana, an old loyalty to Reira and Ren.

## Reira
Register: soft, formal, childlike at times; then a startling flirtatious bluntness that treats feeling as a transaction. English-tinged upbringing, floating quality.
Address: first names, soft; speaks to Takumi as to weather and to Shin as to a little brother in this mode.
Deflects from: her need, by pretending it is a deal or by singing.
Love: possessive, then ashamed. Anger: turned inward.
Never says: that she chose this cage.
Preoccupations: Yasu, being a voice without a person, who she would be without Trapnest.

## Takumi
Register: flat, efficient, brief. Treats a conversation as a meeting already over.
Address: first names, no softness. He calls Hachi "Nana" when everyone else uses the dog's name, and that is the claim. Keeps his contractions to a minimum.
Deflects from: guilt, by being correct. Sidesteps where he has been.
Love: provision and control; he does turn up, and pays, and arranges. Anger: lower and more reasonable than anyone else's.
Humour: rare, cruel, accurate.
Never says: sorry without an invoice attached.
Preoccupations: the band as a machine he built, keeping what is his.

## Naoki
Register: sunny, big-dog friendly, talks with his mouth full. Says the tactless true thing by accident.
Address: nicknames for all. Never scared of Takumi, somehow.
Deflects from: nothing; he simply misses subtext.
Preoccupations: food, drums, everyone getting along.

## Junko
Register: brisk, unsentimental, teasing; the friend who tells you. Warning delivered as a joke.
Address: uses Hachi's real name as a scolding tool.
Love: blunt advice and a spare futon. Anger: sarcasm, then a straight line.
Preoccupations: art, Kyosuke, stopping Hachi walking into traffic.

## Kyosuke
Register: mild, laconic, relaxed. Adoring of Junko in public without embarrassment. Observes from the side.
Preoccupations: painting, noodles, not interfering, then one correct sentence.

## Shoji
Register: earnest, apologetic, gentle; flustered jokes. Avoids confrontation until it is too late.
Address: first names. Guilt colours everything he says to Hachi.
Preoccupations: having been a coward, doing better.

## Misato
Register: bright, deferential, fan-formal; asks permission for everything. Hidden steel and a slightly unsettling devotion.
Address: honorifics, especially toward Nana.
Preoccupations: Nana's wellbeing, being useful, what she is not telling anyone.

## General
Comedy sits right next to heartbreak, often in the same breath. People talk around the thing. Food is love. The retrospective narrator already knows how it ends and forgives everyone.
